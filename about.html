Spatium Novum

Spatium Novum means "a new space". It is where I write about three things I use constantly and understand incompletely: commodity trading, finance, and machine learning.

My background is quantitative research. In practice that means building models of complicated systems, then spending most of the time working out where they break. The habit carries over here. I look for the structure under something that looks opaque, and then pay attention to what that structure fails to explain.

Anchor essays go up roughly every two weeks, with shorter notes in between. If something here is useful, or wrong, I want to hear about it.
