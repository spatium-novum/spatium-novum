Richard Levins American Scientist 1966
Our truth is the intersection of independent lies.

Every model is wrong in its own particular way. Line enough of them up and the overlap starts to look like knowledge.

I build models for a living and read about them the rest of the time: commodities and finance, machine learning, pharmacology, economics, cognition. I have not settled on which of those I will end up working in, so for now I write about all of them, and about the part they share.
